# UK Businesses come from government open data, not commercial directories

In the UK, Businesses are discovered through Companies House and the Food Standards Agency, both published under the Open Government Licence v3.0, alongside OpenStreetMap. The commercial UK directories were each checked and none is used.

The directories were the obvious first choice, because they carry phone numbers and websites that the government registers do not. They were ruled out on their own terms rather than on reachability. FreeIndex's terms of use, read on its own page, state that "the use of FreeIndex data as a source or target for direct marketing, telemarketing, market research, or any other marketing activity ... is strictly prohibited", and forbid using the site "to create, amend, update or verify any databases, directories, customer lists, mailing lists". Yell's terms forbid the same list-building and automated access, and Cloudflare blocks automated requests to it, including to its robots.txt. Thomson Local blocks automated access outright. Robots.txt permitted FreeIndex's listing pages, which is why terms are read as well as robots.txt.

The registers carry the reverse trade-off. Companies House states that it imposes "no rules or requirements on how the information on the public register is used", and the Food Standards Agency's terms restrict only how its hygiene ratings are displayed. Neither gives a way to contact a business: Companies House has no phone or website field, and the Food Standards Agency's phone field was empty for all 500 Norwich businesses sampled. Contact details therefore come from OpenStreetMap and from each Business's own website.

**Consequences**: A UK Business found only through a register shows its website as unknown rather than absent, and stays unscored until something tells us it needs a website. Companies House needs a free API key that a person has to register for. Its address is the registered office, so it runs after the other Sources and only fills fields they leave empty. Pakistan has no equivalent register that can list businesses by trade or place, so it relies on OpenStreetMap alone.
